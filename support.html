Help and FAQ

Common questions about how the app works. If your question isn't here, the contact address is at the bottom.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. ChillHaven is an iPhone app; there is no iPad, Mac, Android or web version.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to remember. Open the app and it works.

Where is my information kept?

In the app's own storage on your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no server. The privacy policy goes through this in detail.

How do I export everything?

Open Settings inside the app and choose "Export data". ChillHaven writes every check-in, sleep entry, routine and reflection to a JSON file, and the standard iOS save sheet lets you choose where it goes.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then "Delete all data". It asks you to confirm, and then removes every entry permanently. Deleting the app from your phone also removes its data, so export first if you want to keep a copy.

Why doesn't it send me notifications?

Because a wellbeing app that nags you is working against itself. You can set a preferred time on a routine as a note to yourself, but this version sends no notifications at all.

Why are there no streaks?

Streaks turn missing a day into a loss, which is the opposite of what this app is for. Routines show a simple count of what you did today, and yesterday has no hold over it.

Does it tell me if something is wrong with me?

No, and it is not built to. Insights describe what you recorded in plain sentences — which day read highest, what your average was. There is no score, no ranking and no diagnosis. If you're worried about your wellbeing, talk to a trusted adult or a qualified health professional.

Can someone else read my entries?

Anyone who can unlock your iPhone can open the app and read what's in it. A device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID is the protection that matters here.

Safety

If you need help right now.

ChillHaven is a personal wellbeing and reflection tool, not a medical or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. If you are worried about your wellbeing, speak with a trusted adult or qualified health professional.

  • Emergency services000Immediate danger, day or night
  • Kids Helpline1800 55 1800Free counselling for people aged 5–25, 24/7
  • Lifeline13 11 14Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7
  • Beyond Blue1300 22 4636Mental wellbeing support, 24/7

These services are Australian. If you are somewhere else, look up the equivalent services for your country.
