Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: ChillHaven collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server to send anything to. The rest of this page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated 14 August 2026

What ChillHaven collects

Nothing. There is no account to create, no email address to give, no analytics package, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting service. The app does not ask for your name unless you choose to type one in for the greeting, and that name never leaves the device either.

What you record, and where it goes

When you record a check-in, a sleep entry, a routine or a reflection, it is written to the app's own storage on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData framework. That storage is part of the app's container on your device.

It is not copied to a server, because ChillHaven does not have one. It is not shared with the developer, and it is not shared with any third party.

Network access

The app does not make network requests to send or receive your wellbeing information. The only links that leave the app are the support links on the safety screen, which open the relevant organisation's own website or dialler in the usual way. Following one of those links is a normal visit to that organisation, governed by their privacy policy rather than this one.

Exporting your data

Settings includes an export that writes everything you have recorded to a JSON file, saved wherever you choose through the standard iOS save sheet. Once you have exported a file, it is an ordinary file on your device or in whichever storage location you picked, and this policy no longer governs it.

Deleting your data

Settings includes a "Delete all data" action that removes every check-in, sleep entry, routine and reflection. It asks for confirmation first, and it cannot be undone.

Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes its storage, and with it everything you recorded. If you want to keep a copy, export it first.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup is made by iOS and may include the app's storage, in the same way it includes other apps' data. Those backups are governed by Apple's terms and your own device settings, not by ChillHaven.

People under 18

ChillHaven is designed for 14–17 year olds. Because the app collects no personal information and transmits nothing, there is no personal information about a young person for anyone to receive, store or misuse.

Wellbeing notes can be private and personal. Anyone with access to an unlocked iPhone can open the app and read what is in it, so a device passcode is the practical protection here.

Not a medical service

ChillHaven is a personal wellbeing and reflection tool, not a medical or emergency service. It does not diagnose anything and does not calculate a mental-health risk score. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. If you are worried about your wellbeing, speak with a trusted adult or a qualified health professional. Support contacts are listed on the support page.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated and the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Questions

Privacy questions can go to the address on the support page.
