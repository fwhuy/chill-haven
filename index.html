About

A wellbeing app should ask for less, not more.

Most wellbeing apps open with a sign-up form. Then come the daily reminders, the streak counter, and a dashboard that quietly turns how you feel into a number going up or down.

ChillHaven started from the opposite question: what is the smallest thing that would still actually help? The answer turned out to be a thirty-second check-in, a handful of calm tools for the moments in between, and an honest description of what you recorded — nothing more.

That shaped every decision after it. There is no account, because there is nothing to sync. There are no streaks, because missing a Tuesday is not a failure. There are no notifications, because an app that nags you about your wellbeing is working against its own purpose.

It is built for 14–17 year olds, and for the adults who have to be comfortable saying yes to it. Both groups ask the same first question — where does this information go? — and the answer is the shortest one we could engineer: nowhere. It stays on the phone.

HH Huy Ho
Co-founder, ChillHaven
What it does

Five things, and nothing else.

Each part does one job. There is no feed, no social layer, and nothing that keeps running once you close the app.

01
Check In

Thirty seconds, then you're done.

Mood, stress and energy on a one-to-five scale, with an optional note about the day.

  • Every scale pairs a number with a written label and its own symbol
  • Optional free-text note and feeling tags
  • Full history, with any entry deletable
02
Sleep

Bedtime, wake time, how it went.

ChillHaven works out the rest, including the nights that run past midnight.

  • Duration calculated for you, wrapping correctly across midnight
  • Quality recorded on the same one-to-five scale
  • Seven-night chart with running averages
03
Routines

No streak to break.

The small things you decided were worth doing, ticked off when they are done.

  • Choose which weekdays each routine applies to
  • Optional preferred time, with no notification attached
  • Progress shown as a count, never as a streak
04
Calm Tools

For when you need to slow down.

Three exercises for the moments when you do not want to record anything at all.

  • Box breathing — four steady stages with a visible cycle count
  • One-minute reset — a short guided pause ending in one next step
  • Reflection — optional written prompts, saved only if you want them
05
Insights

Trends, in ordinary sentences.

Seven and thirty day patterns, written out plainly rather than scored.

  • Mood, stress and energy charted together over your chosen range
  • Plain-language summaries, such as which day read highest
  • No score, no ranking, nothing resembling a diagnosis
Privacy by design

It never leaves your phone.

Not because the app promises not to send anything, but because there is no back end to send it to.

Safety

If you need help right now.

ChillHaven is a personal wellbeing and reflection tool, not a medical or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. If you are worried about your wellbeing, speak with a trusted adult or qualified health professional.
